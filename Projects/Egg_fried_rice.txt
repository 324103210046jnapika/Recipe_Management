Recipe 6: Egg fried rice
Ingredients:
  - 2.0 cups cooked rice
  - 1.0 tablespoon oil
  - 1.0 diced onion
  - 2.0 minced garlic cloves
  - 1.0 cup mixed vegetables
  - 2.0 beaten eggs
  - 1.0 teaspoon soy sauce
  - 1.0 teaspoon oyster sauce
  - 1.0 teaspoon salt 
  - 1.0 teaspoon pepper
  - 1.0 chopped scallions
Instructions:
  1. Heat oil in a large skillet
  2. Add diced onion and minced garlic
  3. Add mixed vegetables
  4. Push vegetables to one side
  5. Add beaten eggs
  6. Mix eggs with vegetables
  7. Add cooked rice
  8. Add soy sauce and oyster sauce
  9. Season with salt and pepper
  10. Garnish with chopped scallions
  11. Serve hot and enjoy

  Calories: 400.0
  Protein: 15.0
  Carbs: 15.0
  Fat: 5.0

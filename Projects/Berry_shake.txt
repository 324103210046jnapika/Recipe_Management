Recipe 1: Berry Shake
Ingredients:
  - 1.0 cup Mixed berries
  - 1.0 cup vanilla yogurt
  - 1.0 cup almond milk
  - 1.0 tablespoon honey
  - 1.0 cup ice cubes
Instructions:

Recipe 4: Chicken Dry Roast
Ingredients:
  - 1.0 lbs Chicken Pieces
  - 3.0 tablespoons vegetable oil
  - 1.0 teaspoon cumin seeds
  - 1.0 teaspoon coriander seeds
  - 1.0 teaspoon fennel seeds
  - 1.0 teaspoon ginger paste
  - 1.0 teaspoon garlic paste
  - 1.0  teaspoon chilli powder
  - 1.0 teaspoon garam masala powder
  - 1.0 teaspoon turmeric powder
  - 1.0 teaspoon salt
Instructions:
  1. Marinate the chicken in spices and yogurt can enhance the flavour
  2. Heat oil in a pan
  3. Add chicken
  4. Cook until done
  5. Continue cooking the chicken until it is dry and roasted ,stirring occasionally
  6. Garnish and Serve

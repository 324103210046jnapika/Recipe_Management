Recipe 5: Carrot Halva
Ingredients:
  - 2.0 cups grated carrots
  - 2.0 cups milk
  - 0.5 cup ghee
  - 0.5 cup sugar
  - 0.25 teaspoon cardamom powder
  - 0.5 teaspoon  saffron seeds
  - 1.0 tablespoon chopped nuts
Instructions:
  1. Heat ghee in a pan over medium heat
  2. Saute the grated carrots
  3. Add milk
  4. Reduce the heat and simmer until the milk is absorbed and carrots are cooked
  5. Add sugar
  6. Cook until thickened
  7. Add cardamom powder and saffron seeds
  8. Garnish with nuts
  9. Serve and Enjoy

  Calories: 270.0

  Carbs: 50.0
  Fat: 14.0

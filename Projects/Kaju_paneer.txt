Recipe 3: Kaju paneer
Ingredients:
  - 250.0 grams Paneer
  - 1.0 cup cashews
  - 1.0 cup fresh cream
  - 1.0 cup tomato puree
  - 1.0 tablespoon butter
  - 1.0 teaspoon cumin seeds
  - 1.0 teaspoon ginger paste
  - 1.0 teaspoon garlic paste
  - 1.0 teaspoon garam masala powder
  - 1.0 teaspoon turmeric powder
  - 1.0 tablespoon salt 
Instructions:
  1. Soak the cashews
  2. Heat butter
  3. Add ginger and garlic paste
  4. Add tomato puree
  5. Blend the soaked cashews into paste
  6. add these ingredients on to a cooker
  7. Add spices
  8. Add paneer
  9. Add cream
  10. Season with salt for a taste
  11. Garnish and Serve
Nutrition Info:
  Calories: 400.0
  Protein: 20.0
  Carbs: 15.0
  Fat: 30.0
